title: Kindness Toward Yourself
aspect: Self-compassion
line: Speak to yourself as you would to a dear friend.
invite: When you catch a harsh thought about yourself, pause and ask: what would I say to a friend in my place?
===
This week we turn toward kindness. Not the kindness we give to others, which many of us find easy. But the kindness we so rarely give ourselves.

Listen to the voice in your head when you make a mistake, or fall behind, or forget something. Often it is sharper than anything we would ever say to a friend. This week, we practise softening that voice. Not to make excuses, but because we grow best in warm soil, not in frost.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by noticing how you are, right now, without judging it.

Perhaps you feel tired. Perhaps hopeful, or tense, or a little numb. Whatever is here, see if you can greet it with the same gentle interest you would offer a friend who has just walked in.
[pause 60]
Now bring to mind someone you love easily. A friend, a child, a grandparent, even a pet. Picture their face. Notice the warmth that rises when you think of them.
[pause 75]
Imagine that this person is going through a hard time. What would you want for them? Perhaps that they feel safe. That they are not too hard on themselves. That they find some ease.

Let those wishes form quietly in your heart.
[pause 75]
Now, slowly, turn those same wishes towards yourself. It may feel strange. That's all right. Silently, say:

May I be gentle with myself. May I be at ease. May I remember that I am doing my best.
[pause 90]
If you notice resistance, a part of you that says you don't deserve this, meet that part kindly too. It is probably very tired. It has been working very hard to keep you safe.

May I be gentle with myself. May I be at ease.
[pause 120]
Rest now in whatever warmth is here, even if it is very small. A small warmth is still warmth.
[pause 120]
This week, here is your invitation. When you catch a harsh thought about yourself, pause and ask: what would I say to a friend in my place? Then try saying that to yourself instead.
[pause 20]
