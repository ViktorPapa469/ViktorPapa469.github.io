title: Seeing the Good
aspect: Appreciation
line: What we look for, we tend to find.
invite: Each day this week, tell one person something you genuinely appreciate about them.
===
This week we practise seeing the good in others. The mind is naturally drawn to problems. It notices what is wrong much faster than what is right. This helped our ancestors stay safe, but it can make us critical, and lonely.

We can train the mind to notice goodness too. Not by ignoring problems, but by giving equal attention to the kindness, effort and beauty in the people around us.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Bring to mind someone you see often. A family member, a friend, a colleague. Picture them as they usually are.
[pause 60]
Now think of one quality you genuinely admire in them. Perhaps their humour, their patience, the way they care for others, the way they work hard without complaint. Let yourself appreciate it fully.
[pause 90]
Now think of someone you find a little difficult. See if you can find just one good quality in them. It might be small. Perhaps they love their family. Perhaps they are honest, even if it's uncomfortable. Perhaps they try hard.

Don't force it. Just look, with curiosity.
[pause 120]
Notice how your feeling towards this person shifts, even slightly, when you see a quality you can appreciate.
[pause 75]
Finally, turn this kind attention towards yourself. What is one good quality you have, that you rarely acknowledge? Let yourself see it, without arguing with it.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I look for the good. Breathing out, I let it be seen.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, tell one person something you genuinely appreciate about them. Be specific. Notice how it lands, for them, and for you.
[pause 20]
[closing]
