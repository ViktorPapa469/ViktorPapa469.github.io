title: Beginner's Mind
aspect: Curiosity
line: Meet familiar things as if for the first time.
invite: Each day, choose one ordinary thing, a cup of tea, a walk, a face, and meet it as if for the first time.
===
This week we practise beginner's mind. It is the attitude of someone seeing a thing for the very first time. Curious. Open. Not yet sure what it is.

As we grow older, we learn to recognise things quickly, and that is useful. But it also means we stop really looking. We see the word tree, and not the tree. We hear the idea of a friend, and not the friend. This week, we let things be new again.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with the breath, since you think you already know it.

Breathe in, and see if you can notice one thing about this breath that you have never noticed before. Its temperature, perhaps. The tiny sound it makes. The exact place where it turns around.
[pause 60]
Now turn your attention to your hands. You have had these hands your whole life. But right now, without looking, what do they actually feel like? Warm or cool? Heavy or light? Is there tingling, or pulsing, or stillness?
[pause 75]
Let your attention move to the sounds around you. Instead of naming them, car, voice, fan, see if you can hear them as pure sound. Rising. Falling. Near. Far. Shapes of sound in the silence.
[pause 90]
Now notice a thought as it arises. Any thought. Instead of following where it wants to take you, look at it with curiosity, the way a child might look at a shell on the beach. Where did it come from? How long does it stay? Where does it go?
[pause 120]
If you find yourself thinking, I've done this before, I know how this goes, let that thought be new too. And return to this moment, which has never happened before, and will never happen again.
[pause 120]
A beginner sees many doors. Someone who is sure they know often sees only one. Rest now in not knowing.
[pause 30]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, choose one ordinary thing, a cup of tea, a walk, a face, and meet it as if for the first time. Look a little longer than you need to. Let it surprise you.
[pause 20]
