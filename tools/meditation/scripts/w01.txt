title: Arriving
aspect: Presence
line: You can only begin from where you are.
invite: Several times a day, before you start something, pause for one breath and notice where you are.
===
This first week is about arriving. Not arriving somewhere better, or calmer, or more finished. Just arriving here, in the life that is already happening.

So much of the day is spent leaning forward, towards the next task, the next message, the next thing that might finally make us feel ready. This week, we practise leaning back, into the present moment, and finding that it can hold us.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Now let's practise arriving, one layer at a time.

First, arrive in the body. Feel where you are touching the ground or the seat. Feel the weight of your hands. There is no need to change anything. Just notice that you are here.
[pause 60]
Next, arrive in the breath. Feel one full breath, from its very beginning to its very end, as if it were the first breath you had ever noticed.
[pause 60]
Now arrive in this moment, exactly as it is. Perhaps there is some tension somewhere. Perhaps a thought keeps tugging at you, about something you have to do later. That's all right. Let the thought be there, and let yourself be here too.

Silently, you might say to yourself: I am here. This is now.
[pause 90]
Whenever you notice the mind has rushed ahead to what comes next, or back to something that has already happened, simply notice where it went. And gently, kindly, arrive again.

I am here. This is now.
[pause 120]
Notice that you do not have to earn this moment. You do not have to be better, or calmer, before you are allowed to be here. You are already here. That is enough to begin.
[pause 120]
Arriving is not something we do once. It is something we do again and again, a thousand times a day. Each time is a small homecoming.
[pause 30]
This week, here is your invitation. Several times a day, before you start something, pause for one breath and notice where you are. Before you open a book. Before you answer the phone. Before you start a focus session. One breath, and a quiet word to yourself: I am here.
[pause 20]
[closing]
