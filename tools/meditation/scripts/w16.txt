title: The Space Between Words
aspect: Deep listening
line: To be truly heard is one of the greatest gifts.
invite: In conversations this week, wait one breath after someone finishes before you reply.
===
This week we return to listening, but now we listen to other people. Often, when someone speaks to us, we are only half there. Part of us is judging, advising, or waiting to tell our own story.

Deep listening means making space. Space for someone to finish. Space for silence. Space for what they really mean, underneath their words. This week, we practise becoming that kind of listener.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's start by listening to silence itself. Between the sounds around you, there are small gaps. Rest your attention in those gaps.
[pause 75]
Now bring to mind a recent conversation, one where you didn't feel you really listened. Perhaps you were distracted, or impatient, or busy thinking of your reply. Don't judge yourself. Just remember it.
[pause 75]
Replay it now in your mind, but this time imagine yourself listening fully. Your body turned towards them. Your mind quiet. Your face soft. Notice what you hear that you might have missed before. Perhaps a tiredness in their voice. Perhaps a worry behind their words.
[pause 120]
Now imagine someone listening to you like that. Not interrupting. Not fixing. Just fully present, letting you say everything you need to say. How does your body feel, imagining being heard in that way?
[pause 120]
This is what we can offer each other. Not answers, very often. Just presence. Just space.
[pause 75]
Breathing in, I make space. Breathing out, I listen.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. In conversations, wait one breath after someone finishes before you reply. Let that breath be a small space, where they can feel that they have truly been heard.
