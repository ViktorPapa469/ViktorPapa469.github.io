title: What Matters Most
aspect: Values
line: When you know what matters, small choices become easy.
invite: Before one decision each day this week, ask: does this move me towards what matters most?
===
Welcome to the fourth and final season of this practice. For these last thirteen weeks, we turn towards meaning. What gives life its depth, and its direction.

We begin with values. Values are not goals we reach. They are like a compass, showing us which way to face. When we know what matters most to us, the many small choices of each day become clearer, and lighter.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with a simple reflection. Imagine you are very old, sitting peacefully, looking back over your life. What would you most want to have spent your time on? Let the answer rise slowly.
[pause 120]
Perhaps words come. Love. Kindness. Learning. Courage. Family. Creativity. Service. Honesty. Let one or two stand out. Don't think too hard. Notice which ones the heart chooses.
[pause 90]
Now think of how you spent your time this past week. Notice, without judgement, where your days matched those values, and where they drifted away.
[pause 120]
Choose one value that feels most alive for you right now. Imagine bringing it into one ordinary moment tomorrow. A conversation, a meal, a piece of work. How might that moment look, if this value guided it?
[pause 120]
Let this value settle into the body, like a warm stone held in the palm.
[pause 60]
Breathing in, I remember what matters. Breathing out, I turn towards it.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Before one decision each day, ask yourself: Does this move me towards what matters most? Let your values be your compass.
[pause 20]
[closing]
