title: Hope
aspect: Looking forward
line: Hope is a small light we carry through the dark.
invite: Each evening this week, write down one thing you are looking forward to, however small.
===
This week, at the end of our season on difficulty, we turn to hope. Hope is not the same as believing everything will turn out perfectly. It is the quiet trust that change is possible, that light can return, that our actions matter.

Even in the hardest times, hope can be as small as a single candle. And a single candle is enough to see the next step.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Imagine sitting in a dark room. And then, imagine someone lighting a single candle in front of you. Watch the small flame. See how much it lights, even though it is tiny.
[pause 90]
Let that flame represent the hope within you. Even on difficult days, it hasn't gone out. Perhaps it flickers. But it is still there.
[pause 90]
Now think back over this season. Facing what is. Courage. Anger. Uncertainty. Grief. Change. The inner critic. Failure. Restlessness. Limits. Balance. Softness. You have met all of these. And you are still here.
[pause 90]
Think of something you are hoping for. It doesn't have to be grand. A better week. A healed relationship. A small dream. Hold it gently, like the candle.
[pause 120]
Ask yourself: What is one small thing I could do to tend this hope? A tiny action that helps the flame burn a little brighter.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I feel the small light. Breathing out, I let it guide me.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Each evening, write down one thing you are looking forward to, however small. Let hope become a habit.
[pause 20]
[closing]
