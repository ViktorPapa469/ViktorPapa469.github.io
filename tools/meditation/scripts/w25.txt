title: Asking for Help
aspect: Humility
line: Needing others is not weakness. It is how we were made.
invite: This week, ask for help with one thing you would normally struggle with alone.
===
This week we look at something many people find very hard: asking for help. We admire independence, and often feel that needing others is a sign of weakness, or that we'll be a burden.

But no one gets through life alone. We were all once babies, completely dependent on others. And we will all need help again. Asking for help is not failure. It is honesty about being human, and it gives others the chance to be generous.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Notice, as you sit here, how much you are already being supported. The floor holds you up. The air feeds you. Your heart beats without asking your permission. You are being helped, right now, in countless ways.
[pause 90]
Now think of a time someone asked you for help, and you were glad to give it. Remember how it felt to be trusted, to be useful to someone.
[pause 75]
Notice that when you hesitate to ask for help, you may be denying someone else that same good feeling.
[pause 60]
Now think of something in your life right now that you are carrying alone. Something that feels heavy, or confusing, or too much. Let yourself admit, gently, that it would be easier with help.
[pause 90]
Who could you ask? A friend, a family member, a colleague, a professional. Let a face come to mind. Imagine asking them, simply and honestly. Notice what you feel.
[pause 120]
If fear or pride comes up, greet it kindly. It's trying to protect you. And then remind yourself: It is all right to need others. We all do.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Ask for help with one thing you would normally struggle with alone. It can be small. Notice what happens when you let someone in.
[pause 20]
[closing]
