title: Looking Back with Love
aspect: Reflection
line: Every season of the year has given you something.
invite: This week, write a short letter to yourself about the year: what you learned, what you are proud of, and what you forgive.
===
This week, as our year of practice draws towards its close, we look back. Not to judge, or count achievements. But to gather the gifts of the year, the way someone gathers a harvest.

Every season has given you something. Some gifts came wrapped in joy. Others came wrapped in difficulty. This week, we honour all of them.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by remembering the first season. Arriving, curiosity, the breath, kindness, patience, rest. Think back to where you were then. Notice how you have changed, even a little.
[pause 90]
Now the second season, with others. Gratitude, loving-kindness, listening, forgiveness, belonging, generosity. Think of the people who have been part of your year. Let yourself feel thankful for them.
[pause 90]
Now the third season, of difficulty. Fear, anger, grief, uncertainty, failure, limits. Think of something hard you faced this year. Notice that you came through it. Let yourself feel some respect for the one who did.
[pause 120]
And this season of meaning. Values, purpose, simplicity, silence, time, awe. What has become clearer to you about what matters?
[pause 120]
Now, gently, offer yourself appreciation for the whole year. The good days and the hard ones. The times you practised, and the times you forgot. Silently say: Thank you. I did my best. I am still growing.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Write a short letter to yourself about the year. What you learned. What you are proud of. And what you forgive.
[pause 20]
[closing]
