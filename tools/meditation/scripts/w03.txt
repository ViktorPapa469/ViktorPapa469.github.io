title: The Breath as Home
aspect: Steadiness
line: Wherever you go, the breath comes with you.
invite: When you feel scattered this week, place a hand on your belly and take three slow breaths before you carry on.
===
This week, we come home to the breath. Of all the things we carry through the day, the breath is the one that never leaves us. It was with you when you woke. It will be with you when you sleep tonight.

When life feels scattered, it can seem as if we have nowhere steady to stand. But the breath is always here, quietly rising and falling, like a house we can always walk back into.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's get to know this home a little better.

Place one hand on your belly, and the other on your chest, if that is comfortable. Feel which hand moves more as you breathe. There is no right answer. Just notice.
[pause 60]
Now let the breath sink a little lower, so that the belly gently rises as you breathe in, and softens as you breathe out. Don't force it. Just invite it.
[pause 75]
Notice that the out-breath can be a little longer than the in-breath. As it lengthens, notice what happens in the shoulders, in the jaw, behind the eyes. A small letting go, each time.
[pause 90]
You can rest your hands down now, and let the breath be completely natural.

Imagine that each in-breath is like opening the front door of your home. And each out-breath is like settling into a familiar chair.
[pause 120]
When the mind wanders off into plans or memories, it's like stepping outside for a moment. That's all right. You know the way back. Just one breath, and you are home again.
[pause 120]
Rest here. Breathing in, I come home. Breathing out, I am here.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. When you feel scattered or rushed, place a hand on your belly and take three slow breaths before you carry on. Let the breath remind you: home is never far away.
[pause 20]
[closing]
