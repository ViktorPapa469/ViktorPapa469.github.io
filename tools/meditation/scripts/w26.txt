title: Being Heard
aspect: Self-expression
line: Your voice matters. Let it be heard.
invite: This week, share one honest feeling with someone you trust, out loud or in writing.
===
This week, the last of our second season, we turn to being heard. Earlier we practised listening to others. Now we honour our own voice.

Many of us learned, at some point, to keep quiet. To hide our feelings, our opinions, our needs, so as not to cause trouble. But what we hide doesn't disappear. It waits inside us. This week, we practise letting ourselves be heard, first by ourselves, and then by others.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by listening inward. Ask yourself, gently: What do I need to say, that I haven't said? It might be to someone else. It might be to yourself. Just let the question rest there, and see what arises.
[pause 120]
Whatever came up, notice how it feels in the body. Perhaps a lump in the throat. A tightness in the chest. A sense of something wanting to be released.
[pause 75]
Bring your attention to the throat. The place where breath becomes voice. Let it soften. Imagine the breath moving freely through it, with nothing blocking the way.
[pause 90]
Now imagine speaking what you need to say, in a safe place, to someone who truly listens. They don't interrupt. They don't judge. They simply receive your words with care.

Notice what it feels like to be heard.
[pause 120]
Your feelings, your thoughts, your needs, are valid. They deserve a place in the world. You do not have to shout. But you don't have to disappear either.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I listen to myself. Breathing out, I let myself be heard.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Share one honest feeling with someone you trust, out loud, or in writing. Start small. Let your voice take up a little more room.
[pause 20]
