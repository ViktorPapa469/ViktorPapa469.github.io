title: Equanimity
aspect: Balance
line: Like a mountain in changing weather, stay steady at the centre.
invite: This week, when things go well or badly, pause and notice: this too will pass. Let your centre stay steady.
===
This week we practise equanimity. Equanimity is a steady, balanced mind. Not cold, or uncaring. But calm enough to stay present through ups and downs, without being thrown around by every change.

Life brings praise and blame, gain and loss, pleasure and pain. We cannot control which comes. But we can learn to stay steady at the centre, like a mountain that remains itself through sun, rain, wind and snow.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Imagine a great mountain. Its base is wide and rooted deep in the earth. Its peak rises into the sky. Let yourself feel as if your body were that mountain. Solid. Grounded. Still.
[pause 90]
Now imagine the weather changing around the mountain. Sunshine warms it. Clouds cover it. Rain falls, wind blows, snow settles on its slopes. Through it all, the mountain remains.
[pause 90]
Now bring to mind a pleasant event from recent days. Notice how the mind leans towards it, wanting more. Then let it pass, like sunshine on the mountain.
[pause 90]
Now bring to mind something unpleasant. Notice how the mind pulls away, wanting it gone. Let that pass too, like a storm over the mountain.
[pause 90]
Feel the steadiness underneath both. The part of you that watches, without being swept away.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I am steady. Breathing out, I let the weather pass.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. When things go well, or badly, pause and remember: This too will pass. Feel the mountain in you, and let your centre stay steady.
[pause 20]
[closing]
