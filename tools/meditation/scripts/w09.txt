title: Letting Go
aspect: Release
line: An open hand can receive what a closed fist cannot.
invite: Each night this week, before sleep, name one thing from the day you are ready to set down, and breathe it out.
===
This week we practise letting go. We hold on to so many things. Old conversations. Plans that did not work out. The way we think things should be. Often we don't even notice how tightly we are gripping, until our hands ache.

Letting go is not the same as not caring. It is choosing to stop carrying what no longer needs to be carried, so that our hands are free for what is here now.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with the hands themselves. Gently make two fists. Not too hard. Just enough to feel the grip.

Hold them for a few breaths. Notice the effort it takes.
[pause 20]
And now, slowly, open them. Let the fingers uncurl. Rest the hands, palms up if you like, on your lap.

Feel the difference. The ease of an open hand.
[pause 60]
Now, with each out-breath, let something soften. On this breath, the forehead.
[pause 20]
On this breath, the shoulders.
[pause 20]
On this breath, the belly. Let it be soft and round.
[pause 60]
Now bring to mind something small that you have been holding on to. A minor worry, an irritation, a thing you keep replaying. Don't choose the heaviest thing. Start with something light.

Notice how it feels to hold it. Where does it sit in the body?
[pause 90]
Now imagine placing it in your open hands, like a leaf. You don't have to throw it away. You don't have to solve it. Just let it rest there, not gripped.

And if it's ready, imagine a gentle breeze lifting it away. If it's not ready, that's fine too. Let it stay on your open palm.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I notice. Breathing out, I loosen my grip.
[pause 120]
This week, here is your invitation. Each night before you sleep, name one thing from the day that you are ready to set down. Then breathe it out, and let the night hold it for you.
[pause 20]
[closing]
