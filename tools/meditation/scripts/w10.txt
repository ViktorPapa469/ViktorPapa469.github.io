title: Small Things
aspect: Wonder
line: The ordinary is full of quiet miracles.
invite: Each day, find one small thing worth wondering at, and give it a full minute of attention.
===
This week we turn towards small things. We often wait for big moments to feel wonder. A mountain view, a birth, a great success. But wonder is hiding everywhere, in the ordinary.

The way light falls on a wall in the afternoon. The steam rising from a cup. The fact that your heart has beaten, without a single break, for your entire life. This week, we practise noticing the small miracles that are always here.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with the smallest thing you can feel. Notice the very tip of your nose, where the air enters. Feel how cool it is on the way in. How warm on the way out.
[pause 60]
Now notice the tips of your fingers. Can you feel them, without moving them? Perhaps a faint pulse, a gentle tingling. These tiny sensations are always here, quietly, whether we notice them or not.
[pause 75]
Consider the breath itself. With each breath, air that has travelled around the whole earth enters your body. The oxygen that trees and plants released is now becoming part of you. Just sit with that for a moment.
[pause 90]
Now think of one small thing you saw or touched today. A leaf, a cup, a door handle, someone's smile. Bring it to mind in as much detail as you can. Its colour, its shape, its texture.
[pause 90]
Let a little wonder arise. Not because this thing is special, but because it exists at all. That there is anything, rather than nothing.
[pause 120]
Rest in that gentle amazement. Breathing in, I notice. Breathing out, I wonder.
[pause 120]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, find one small thing worth wondering at. A raindrop, an insect, a crack in the pavement. Give it a full minute of your attention. Let the ordinary surprise you.
[pause 20]
[closing]
