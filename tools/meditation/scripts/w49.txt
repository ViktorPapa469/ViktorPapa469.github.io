title: Service
aspect: Giving beyond ourselves
line: A life spent only on ourselves is too small a home.
invite: This week, do one act of service for someone who cannot repay you.
===
This week we reflect on service. When we are anxious or unhappy, our world tends to shrink. We think mostly about ourselves, our problems, our needs. And the smaller our world becomes, the heavier it feels.

Serving others opens the world up again. Not as a duty, and not to feel superior, but because helping is one of the most natural and satisfying things a human being can do. It reminds us that we belong to something larger.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by remembering someone who served you without expecting anything in return. A parent, a teacher, a nurse, a neighbour, a stranger. Let yourself feel again the care they gave.
[pause 90]
Notice that their kindness is still alive in you. It has shaped who you are. Service ripples outwards in ways we can never fully see.
[pause 90]
Now think of the people and places around you. Your home, your street, your workplace, your community. Where is there a need? Perhaps someone lonely. Someone overwhelmed. A place that needs care.
[pause 120]
Imagine offering something small. An hour of your time. A meal. A listening ear. A skill you have. Notice how it feels to imagine giving it freely.
[pause 120]
Let the wish to be of use settle in your heart, gently, without pressure. You don't need to save the world. Just to be kind to the part of it in front of you.
[pause 60]
Breathing in, I receive the care of others. Breathing out, I pass it on.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Do one act of service for someone who cannot repay you. Then notice how it changes your own day.
[pause 20]
[closing]
