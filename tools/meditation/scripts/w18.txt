title: Interconnection
aspect: Belonging to the whole
line: Nothing exists on its own. We are woven together.
invite: At each meal this week, pause to think of everyone and everything that brought the food to your plate.
===
This week we reflect on interconnection. We often feel like separate individuals, each alone inside our own skin. But look closely at anything, and you will find it connected to everything else.

The breath you take was breathed out by trees. The water in your body fell as rain on distant mountains. Your thoughts are shaped by words you learned from others. This week, we feel into this web of connection, and our place within it.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's start with the breath. As you breathe in, remember that this air has travelled. It has moved across oceans and forests. It has been breathed by countless living things before you.
[pause 75]
As you breathe out, remember that your breath goes on, too. Plants will take it in. It becomes part of the living world again. You are constantly giving and receiving, simply by breathing.
[pause 90]
Now think of something you are wearing. Your shirt, perhaps. Think of where the cotton grew, the people who picked it, who spun it, who sewed it, who carried it across the world to you. All these lives, touching yours.
[pause 120]
Now think of the people in your own life. Family, friends, colleagues. Each of them carries a little of you inside them, and you carry a little of each of them. A phrase, a habit, a memory.
[pause 120]
Rest now in the feeling of being held by this vast web. You are not as alone as you sometimes feel. You never were.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I receive from the world. Breathing out, I give back to it.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. At each meal, pause to think of everyone and everything that brought the food to your plate. The sun, the rain, the soil, the many hands. Eat with that connection in mind.
[pause 20]
[closing]
