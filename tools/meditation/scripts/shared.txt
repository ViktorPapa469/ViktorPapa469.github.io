## opening A
Welcome. Take a moment to find a way of sitting that is steady and comfortable. On a chair, on a cushion, or lying down, if that serves you better today.

Let your spine be upright, but not stiff. Let your hands rest wherever they are easy.

When you are ready, let your eyes close. Or let your gaze fall softly to the floor in front of you.
[pause 8]
Take one slow breath in through the nose. And let it go, a little longer on the way out.
[pause 6]
Once more. In. And out.
[pause 6]
And now let the breath return to its own rhythm. You don't need to manage it. It has been breathing you all day.
[pause 15]
For the next twenty minutes, there is nothing to fix and nowhere to get to. You have already arrived.
[pause 20]

## opening B
Hello, and welcome. Let's begin by simply arriving.

Settle into your seat. Feel the weight of your body being held, by the chair, the cushion, the floor beneath you.

Let your shoulders drop away from your ears. Unclench the jaw. Let the space between your eyebrows grow wide and smooth.
[pause 10]
Close your eyes, or lower them.

Notice the sounds around you. Near sounds. Far sounds. You don't need to name them or push them away. Let them come and go, like weather.
[pause 20]
Now gather your attention a little closer, to the feeling of breathing. The air moving in. The air moving out.
[pause 20]
Whatever kind of day it has been, you can set it down beside you for now. It will still be there later, if you need it.
[pause 15]

## opening C
Welcome to this time you have set aside for yourself.

Begin by letting your body settle. Sit tall, as if a thread were lifting the crown of your head, while everything below it softens and grows heavy.
[pause 8]
Let your eyes close.

Take three slow breaths at your own pace. With each out-breath, let something go. The first one, let go of the rush that brought you here.
[pause 10]
The second, let go of any effort to do this well.
[pause 10]
And the third, simply let go.
[pause 12]
Now let your breathing be natural again. Notice how your body feels, just as it is, in this moment. Tired, or restless, or calm. All of it is welcome here.
[pause 20]

## opening D
Welcome back to the cushion, or the chair, or wherever you are sitting today.

Take a moment to adjust your posture. Feet resting on the ground. Back supported by its own strength. Hands relaxed.

Close your eyes when you feel ready.
[pause 8]
Let's take a brief tour of the body. Notice your feet, and the ground beneath them.
[pause 6]
Your legs, and the places where they touch the seat.
[pause 6]
Your belly, rising and falling.
[pause 6]
Your hands. Your shoulders. Your face.
[pause 8]
And now the whole body at once, sitting here, breathing.
[pause 15]
Nothing is required of you right now. Only that you stay, and notice.
[pause 15]

## anchor A
Let's steady the mind before we go further. Bring your attention to the breath, wherever you feel it most clearly. Perhaps at the tip of the nose. Perhaps in the rise and fall of the chest, or the belly.

Rest your attention there, lightly, the way a hand might rest on a sleeping animal.
[pause 30]
When you notice that the mind has wandered, and it will, that moment of noticing is the practice. Gently, without any scolding, come back to the breath.
[pause 45]
In. And out. Just this breath.
[pause 45]

## anchor B
For the next few minutes, let's count the breath to help the mind settle.

Breathe in, and as you breathe out, silently count one. Breathe in, and on the out-breath, two. Continue up to five, and then begin again at one.

If you lose count, or find yourself at eleven, just smile and begin again at one. Losing count is not failing. It is how we learn.
[pause 60]
Keep counting, gently. One to five. And back to one.
[pause 60]
Now let the counting fall away, and simply rest with the breath.
[pause 20]

## anchor C
Let's give the mind a place to rest. Notice the very start of each in-breath, as it begins. Notice its middle, and its end.

Notice the small pause before the out-breath begins. And the out-breath itself, from its beginning to its end.
[pause 40]
Each breath is new. You have never breathed this one before.
[pause 50]
If thoughts arrive, let them pass by like clouds crossing the sky. You are the sky. Return to the breath.
[pause 50]

## closing A
Now, slowly, let's begin to come back.

Let your breathing deepen a little. Feel the weight of your body on the seat.

Bring a small movement into your fingers and toes. Perhaps roll your shoulders, or turn your head gently from side to side.
[pause 10]
Before you open your eyes, take a moment to thank yourself for taking this time. It is not a small thing.
[pause 8]
When you are ready, let your eyes open, and take in the room around you. Carry a little of this stillness with you, into whatever comes next.
[pause 5]

## closing B
Let's start to bring this practice to a close.

Take a slightly deeper breath in. And a long, easy breath out.

Notice how you feel now, compared with when you sat down. There's no right answer. Just notice.
[pause 10]
Begin to move your body in whatever way feels good. A stretch. A gentle sway.
[pause 8]
And when you are ready, open your eyes. The week is waiting, and you can meet it from here.
[pause 5]

## closing C
Slowly, let your attention widen again. Hear the sounds in the room. Feel the air on your skin.

Let a little movement return to your hands and feet.
[pause 8]
You may like to place a hand over your heart for a moment, and simply rest it there.
[pause 10]
Thank you for practising today. Whenever you are ready, open your eyes, and go gently.
[pause 5]

## closing D
It is time to return.

Take one full breath, filling up from the belly to the chest. And let it go with a sigh, if you like.
[pause 6]
Wiggle your fingers. Stretch your arms, or your back, in whatever way your body asks for.
[pause 8]
Remember, this stillness is not something you have to come here to find. It is always just one breath away.

When you feel ready, open your eyes, and welcome back.
[pause 5]
