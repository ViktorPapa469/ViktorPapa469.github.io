title: The Inner Critic
aspect: Self-talk
line: You can hear the critic without believing every word.
invite: When the inner critic speaks this week, name it gently, "there's the critic", and then ask what a kind coach would say instead.
===
This week we meet the inner critic. Most of us have a voice inside that judges us. You're not good enough. You should have done better. Everyone else has it together.

This voice often began as an attempt to protect us, to keep us safe from rejection or failure. But over time it can become harsh, and exhausting. This week, we learn to hear the critic without automatically believing it.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's start by just noticing thoughts. Imagine your mind is a sky, and thoughts are clouds drifting across it. You don't need to follow them. Just notice them passing.
[pause 90]
Now, gently, invite the inner critic to speak. What does it often say to you? Let a typical phrase arise. Notice its tone. Is it sharp? Cold? Impatient?
[pause 75]
Notice how your body responds when you hear it. Perhaps you shrink a little. Perhaps your shoulders tense.
[pause 60]
Now, instead of arguing with the critic, try naming it. Silently say: Ah, there's the critic again. Notice what happens when you see it as a voice, rather than the truth.
[pause 90]
Ask yourself: What would a kind coach say instead? Someone who believes in you, who wants you to grow, but speaks with warmth. Let that voice speak to you now. What does it say?
[pause 120]
The critic may not disappear. That's all right. You don't have to silence it. You simply don't have to let it be the only voice you listen to.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I hear the critic. Breathing out, I choose a kinder voice.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. When the inner critic speaks, name it gently: there's the critic. Then ask what a kind coach would say instead, and listen to that.
[pause 20]
[closing]
