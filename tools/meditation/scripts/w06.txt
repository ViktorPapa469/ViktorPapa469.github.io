title: Patience
aspect: Waiting well
line: Some things open only in their own time.
invite: When you find yourself waiting this week, in a queue, for a reply, for a page to load, use it as a moment to breathe instead of a moment to rush.
===
This week we practise patience. We live in a world that has trained us to expect everything instantly. Answers, messages, results. And so ordinary waiting has started to feel like a small injury.

But many of the most important things in life grow slowly. Trust. Skill. Healing. A seed does not open faster because we stare at it. This week, we practise the quiet art of waiting well.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by noticing the urge to hurry, even here.

Perhaps a part of you is already wondering how long this will take, or what you will do afterwards. Just notice that urge, without obeying it. Feel where it lives in the body. Perhaps a tightness in the chest, or a restlessness in the legs.
[pause 75]
Now breathe gently into that place. Not to push the feeling away. Just to keep it company.
[pause 75]
Imagine a seed lying under the earth, in the cool and the dark. It does not know when it will break the surface. It simply does what it can, each day, and trusts the season.

Let yourself be like that seed for a while. Doing nothing, and still, quietly, growing.
[pause 120]
Each time the mind jumps ahead, to the next task, to the end of this session, notice it kindly, and return to the breath. Patience is not something we have. It is something we practise, one return at a time.
[pause 120]
Think of something in your life that is unfolding slowly right now. A goal, a relationship, your own healing. See if you can offer it a little more time. Silently, you might say: It is all right to grow slowly.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. When you find yourself waiting, in a queue, for a reply, for a page to load, use it as a moment to breathe instead of a moment to rush. Let waiting become a small meditation.
