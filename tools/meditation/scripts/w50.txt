title: Awe
aspect: Vastness
line: In the presence of something vast, our worries find their true size.
invite: Each night this week, step outside and look up at the sky for a few minutes before you sleep.
===
This week we open to awe. Awe is the feeling we have when we meet something vast. A night sky full of stars. An ocean stretching to the horizon. A mountain range. A newborn's tiny hand.

In awe, the busy, worried self grows quiet. Our problems don't disappear, but they find their true size, within something much larger. And many people find that awe leaves them calmer, kinder, and more connected.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Imagine lying on your back on a warm night, far from any city lights. Above you, the sky is full of stars. Thousands of them. More than you could ever count.
[pause 90]
Let your gaze widen to take in the whole sky. Some of the light you see left those stars long before you were born. It has travelled all this way, just to reach your eyes tonight.
[pause 120]
Feel how small you are beneath this sky. And at the same time, notice that you are part of it. Made of the same elements as the stars. Breathing on a planet turning slowly through space.
[pause 120]
Now bring to mind a worry you have been carrying. Hold it up against this vast sky. Notice its size. It may still matter. But perhaps it is a little lighter now.
[pause 120]
Rest in the vastness. Let it hold you.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I open to the vast. Breathing out, I rest within it.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Each night, step outside and look up at the sky for a few minutes before you sleep. Let awe be the last feeling of your day.
[pause 20]
[closing]
