title: Listening
aspect: Attention
line: Attention is the simplest form of love.
invite: In one conversation each day, listen without planning your reply. Let the other person finish completely.
===
This week we practise listening. Real listening is rarer than it seems. Most of the time, while someone else is speaking, we are already preparing what we will say next.

But listening is one of the deepest things we can offer, to other people, and to the world around us. When we truly listen, the mind grows quiet, and something in us opens. This week we listen to sound, to the body, and to the silence underneath it all.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let the breath fade into the background now, and open your attention to sound.

Begin with the sounds that are furthest away. Perhaps traffic, or birds, or wind, or the hum of a building. Let your hearing reach out, as far as it can go.
[pause 75]
Now bring your listening closer. The sounds in the room you are in. A clock, perhaps, or the soft rustle of your own clothes.
[pause 75]
And now closer still. The sound of your own breathing. Perhaps even the faint sound of your heartbeat, or the quiet ringing of silence in your ears.
[pause 90]
Notice how sounds arise all by themselves. You don't have to go and fetch them. They arrive, they stay for a while, and they fade. You can simply receive them.
[pause 120]
Now listen to the spaces between sounds. The silence that each sound comes out of, and returns to. Rest your attention there for a while.
[pause 120]
Finally, listen inward, to how you are feeling. Not to the story about it. Just the feeling itself, as if it were a sound you were hearing for the first time.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. In one conversation each day, listen without planning your reply. Let the other person finish completely. Notice what you hear when you are no longer waiting for your turn.
