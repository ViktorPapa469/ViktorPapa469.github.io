title: Beginning Again
aspect: Resilience
line: Every moment is a fresh place to start.
invite: Whenever you slip this week, a broken habit, a lost temper, a wasted hour, say quietly: begin again. Then do the next small thing.
===
This week marks the end of the first season of this practice. Thirteen weeks. And so it is a good moment to talk about beginning again.

Perhaps you have practised every week. Perhaps you have missed many. Either way, you are here now. And here is always a place we can begin. Resilience is not about never falling. It is about how gently, and how quickly, we stand up again.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin, as we always do, with this breath. Notice that it is a new breath. The last one is already gone. It cannot be improved, and it cannot be ruined. Only this one is here.
[pause 75]
Each time you have wandered during these meditations and come back, you have practised beginning again. Hundreds of times now. Perhaps thousands. That is a real strength you have been building, even when it felt like failing.
[pause 90]
Now think of something in your life where you have stumbled. A habit you let slip. A promise to yourself you didn't keep. Notice any heaviness, or disappointment, that comes with it.
[pause 75]
Instead of turning away from that feeling, breathe with it for a moment. And then, as gently as you would speak to a child who has fallen over, say to yourself: That's all right. Let's begin again.
[pause 120]
Notice that beginning again doesn't erase what happened. It simply refuses to let what happened decide what comes next.
[pause 120]
Look back over these thirteen weeks. Arriving. Curiosity. The breath as home. Kindness. Listening. Patience. Enough. The body. Letting go. Small things. Steady effort. Rest. Choose one that you would like to carry into the next season.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Whenever you slip, a broken habit, a lost temper, a wasted hour, say quietly: Begin again. Then do the next small thing. Nothing more is needed.
[pause 20]
