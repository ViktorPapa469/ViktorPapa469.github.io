title: Forgiveness
aspect: Release of resentment
line: Forgiveness is a gift we give ourselves first.
invite: This week, notice one small resentment and, each day, practise wishing that person, and yourself, a little peace.
===
This week we approach forgiveness, gently and slowly. Forgiveness does not mean saying that what happened was acceptable. It doesn't mean we must reconcile, or forget, or let someone hurt us again.

It means choosing, over time, to stop carrying the weight of resentment in our own hearts. Because that weight hurts us most. This week, we take only small steps. There is no hurry. And you can stop at any point that feels like too much.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with forgiveness towards yourself, which is often the hardest. Think of a small mistake you made recently. Something you still feel a little bad about.

Notice how you have been holding it. Perhaps as a tightness, or a heaviness.
[pause 75]
Now place a hand on your heart, if you like, and say quietly: I made a mistake. I am human. I forgive myself, as much as I can today.
[pause 90]
Now bring to mind someone who has hurt you in a small way. Not the deepest wound in your life. Something minor. A sharp word, a broken promise.

Notice what arises. Anger, perhaps. Sadness. A wish that they had acted differently. Let all of it be here.
[pause 90]
Now see if you can remember that this person, too, is human. They too act from their own pain, their own fears, their own limits. This doesn't excuse them. It just lets you see them more fully.
[pause 90]
If you feel ready, silently say: I am willing to begin letting this go. For my own peace. If you're not ready, that is completely fine. Simply say: I am willing to be willing.
[pause 120]
Rest now in the breath. Notice whatever has softened, even a little. And notice whatever has not, without judgement.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Notice one small resentment, and each day, practise wishing that person, and yourself, a little peace. Forgiveness grows slowly, like healing. Be patient with it.
[pause 20]
[closing]
