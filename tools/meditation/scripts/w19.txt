title: Generosity
aspect: Giving freely
line: What we give with an open hand comes back as joy.
invite: Each day this week, give something small without being asked: your time, a compliment, a helping hand.
===
This week we explore generosity. We often think of generosity as giving money or gifts. But it is much wider than that. A patient ear is generous. So is a sincere compliment, or letting someone go ahead of you in a queue.

Generosity is also an inner attitude. A loosening of the grip of me and mine. And the surprising thing is, it often brings more joy to the giver than to the one who receives.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by remembering a time someone was generous to you. Perhaps a small kindness you never expected. Let yourself feel again what it was like to receive it.
[pause 75]
Now remember a time you gave something freely. Not out of duty, but because you wanted to. Notice the feeling that came with it. Perhaps warmth, or lightness, or quiet happiness.
[pause 90]
Notice that this feeling is available to you any time. You don't need much to be generous. A kind word costs nothing. Attention costs nothing. A smile costs nothing.
[pause 90]
Now think of someone in your life who could use a little generosity right now. Perhaps someone who is struggling, or lonely, or just tired. What is one small thing you could offer them this week?

Let the idea form gently. You don't need to decide now.
[pause 120]
Finally, be generous with yourself. Give yourself the gift of this moment, without hurrying on to the next thing. Let it be complete.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I open my hands. Breathing out, I give freely.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, give something small without being asked. Your time, a compliment, a helping hand. Notice how it feels to give without expecting anything in return.
[pause 20]
[closing]
