title: Nature as Teacher
aspect: Learning from the living world
line: The trees have been patient far longer than we have.
invite: Spend at least ten minutes outdoors each day this week, walking slowly, noticing one lesson nature offers.
===
This week we turn to nature as our teacher. For thousands of years, people have looked to rivers, trees, mountains and seasons for wisdom. Nature doesn't hurry, and yet everything is accomplished.

A tree grows slowly and stands firm. A river finds its way around every obstacle. Seasons change without resistance. This week, we let the living world remind us how to live.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Imagine you are sitting beneath a large, old tree. Feel its shade. Hear the leaves moving in the breeze above you. Feel the ground beneath you, where its roots spread wide.
[pause 90]
Consider the tree's patience. It has stood here through many seasons. Storms, droughts, long winters. It doesn't complain. It simply grows, slowly, a little each year.

What might you learn from its patience?
[pause 120]
Now imagine a river flowing nearby. When it meets a rock, it doesn't fight. It finds a way around, or slowly wears the rock smooth. It keeps moving towards the sea.

What might you learn from its persistence?
[pause 120]
Now feel the season that is around you now. The light, the temperature, the length of the day. Nature is always changing, and never in a rush.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I root like the tree. Breathing out, I flow like the river.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Spend at least ten minutes outdoors each day, walking slowly. Notice one lesson nature offers you. A bird, a cloud, a leaf, the wind.
[pause 20]
[closing]
