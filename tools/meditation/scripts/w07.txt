title: Enough
aspect: Contentment
line: What if this, right now, were enough?
invite: Each evening, name three things from the day that were enough just as they were.
===
This week we sit with a simple, surprising word: enough. So much of life is driven by the feeling that something is missing. Not enough time, not enough done, not enough of whatever we think will finally let us rest.

Contentment doesn't mean giving up on hopes or goals. It means noticing that, alongside all our wanting, there is already a great deal here. This week we practise letting this moment be enough, just for a while.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with the body. Notice that, right now, you are breathing without effort. Air arrives when you need it. Is this breath enough?
[pause 60]
Notice that you are being held, by the chair or the floor. You don't have to hold yourself up entirely on your own. Is this support enough?
[pause 60]
Notice the simple fact that you are here, aware, alive. Not as an achievement, but as a gift that arrived without asking.
[pause 90]
Now bring to mind something you have been wanting. Perhaps something small, perhaps something big. Feel the pull of it in your body. The leaning forward.

You don't have to give up this wish. Just notice what it feels like to want. And then, gently, let your attention return to what is already here.
[pause 120]
Silently, you might say to yourself: For now, this is enough. I am enough.

Notice how the body responds to those words. Perhaps a softening. Perhaps resistance. Both are welcome.
[pause 120]
Rest in the simple sufficiency of this breath. And this one.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Each evening, before you sleep, name three things from the day that were enough just as they were. A meal. A moment of quiet. A kind word. Let the day end in fullness, rather than in lack.
[pause 20]
