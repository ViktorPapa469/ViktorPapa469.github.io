title: Facing What Is
aspect: Acceptance
line: Peace begins when we stop arguing with what is already here.
invite: When something difficult happens this week, say quietly: this is how it is right now. Then ask what the next wise step might be.
===
Welcome to the third season of this practice. For the next thirteen weeks, we turn towards difficulty. Fear, anger, loss, uncertainty. The parts of life we usually try to avoid.

We begin with acceptance. Acceptance doesn't mean liking something, or giving up. It means seeing clearly what is already true, so that we can respond wisely, instead of wasting our strength wishing it were otherwise.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with something small. Notice any discomfort in the body right now. An itch, a stiffness, a feeling of tiredness. Instead of shifting to get rid of it straight away, stay with it for a few breaths.
[pause 60]
Notice the difference between the sensation itself, and your resistance to it. The sensation is just a feeling. The resistance adds a layer: I don't like this, I want it to stop.
[pause 75]
Now see if you can soften the resistance, even a little. Say to the sensation: You can be here. I'm not fighting you.
[pause 90]
Now bring to mind a situation in your life that you wish were different. Not the hardest thing. Something manageable. Notice the part of you that keeps arguing with it. It shouldn't be like this.
[pause 90]
Gently, see if you can say instead: This is how it is, right now. Notice how the body responds. Perhaps a little settling. Perhaps sadness. Both are welcome.
[pause 120]
From this place of seeing clearly, ask yourself: What is one wise step I could take? Not to fix everything. Just one step.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I see what is. Breathing out, I make peace with this moment.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. When something difficult happens, say quietly: This is how it is right now. Then ask what the next wise step might be.
[pause 20]
