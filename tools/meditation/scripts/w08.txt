title: The Body Remembers
aspect: Embodiment
line: The body is always in the present moment. Visit it often.
invite: Three times a day, pause and ask your body: how are you? Then listen for the answer before you move on.
===
This week we return to the body. The mind travels everywhere, to yesterday, to tomorrow, to places we have never been. But the body is always here, always now.

Many of us live mostly from the neck up, and only notice the body when it complains. Yet it carries so much wisdom. Tiredness, tension, ease and joy all show up in the body first. This week, we learn to listen to it.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's move slowly through the body, like a gentle light passing over it. Wherever the attention rests, simply notice what is there. Warmth, coolness, tingling, pressure, or nothing at all. Nothing at all is fine too.

Begin with the feet. The soles, the toes, the heels.
[pause 45]
Let the attention rise to the ankles, the shins and calves, the knees.
[pause 45]
The thighs, and the hips, and the places where your body meets the seat.
[pause 45]
The belly, rising and falling with the breath. The lower back. The whole length of the spine.
[pause 60]
The chest. The heart, beating quietly on its own. The shoulders, which carry so much for us.
[pause 60]
Down the arms, to the elbows, the wrists, the hands, and each finger.
[pause 45]
The neck. The jaw. The mouth and tongue. The cheeks, the eyes, the forehead. The top of the head.
[pause 60]
And now feel the whole body together, breathing, alive, here.
[pause 90]
If there is any place that feels tight or tired, send it a little gratitude. It has been working hard for you. Silently say: Thank you. I am listening.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Three times a day, pause and ask your body: how are you? Then listen for the answer before you move on. Maybe it needs water, or a stretch, or simply to be noticed.
