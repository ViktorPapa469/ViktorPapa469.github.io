title: Being Real
aspect: Authenticity
line: The world needs you, not a copy of someone else.
invite: This week, notice one moment each day when you are pretending, and try being a little more yourself.
===
This week we reflect on being real. From an early age, we learn to adjust ourselves to fit in. We show the parts others will approve of, and hide the rest. Over time, we can lose touch with who we really are.

Being real means letting our outside match our inside, a little more each day. It doesn't mean saying everything we think. It means not pretending to be someone we are not. And it brings a deep kind of rest.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by simply being here, as you are. Not as you think you should be in meditation. Not calmer, not wiser. Just you, breathing, right now.
[pause 90]
Notice how it feels to stop performing, even for a moment. No one is watching. There is no one to impress.
[pause 90]
Now think of a situation where you often wear a mask. Perhaps at work, perhaps with certain people, perhaps online. What do you show? What do you hide?
[pause 90]
Notice how much energy the mask takes. And notice that you are not wrong for wearing it. It has helped you. But perhaps you no longer need it quite so much.
[pause 90]
Imagine showing up in that situation as yourself, a little more honest, a little more relaxed. Notice what happens in the body as you imagine it.
[pause 120]
Silently, say to yourself: It is safe to be myself here. I am enough as I am.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Notice one moment each day when you are pretending, and try being a little more yourself. Small steps are enough.
[pause 20]
[closing]
