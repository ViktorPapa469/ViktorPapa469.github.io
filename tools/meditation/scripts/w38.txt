title: Strength in Softness
aspect: Vulnerability
line: Water is soft, and yet it shapes the stone.
invite: This week, let one person see a little more of the real you: a worry, a hope, a need.
===
This week we explore strength in softness. We often think strength means being hard, never showing doubt or hurt. But the strongest things in nature are often soft. Water wears away rock. Roots crack through stone. Bamboo bends in the storm and does not break.

Allowing ourselves to be soft, to be seen, to admit we don't have all the answers, takes real courage. And it is often the doorway to deeper connection.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by softening the body. Let the forehead be smooth. Let the eyes rest in their sockets. Let the jaw hang loose. Let the belly be soft and round.
[pause 75]
Notice whether softening feels safe, or whether part of you wants to stay on guard. Both are understandable. Just notice.
[pause 60]
Now imagine a stream flowing over stones. The water is gentle, and yet over time, it shapes everything it touches. Let yourself be like that water. Soft, flowing, patient, and quietly powerful.
[pause 120]
Think of a part of yourself you usually hide. A fear, a need, a tender hope. Something you rarely let others see. Hold it gently in your awareness, as you might hold a small bird.
[pause 90]
Imagine sharing it with someone who would receive it kindly. Notice what it would feel like to be seen in this softer way. Perhaps frightening. Perhaps a relief.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I let myself be soft. Breathing out, I trust my quiet strength.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Let one person see a little more of the real you. A worry, a hope, a need. Soft enough to be honest. Strong enough to stay.
[pause 20]
[closing]
