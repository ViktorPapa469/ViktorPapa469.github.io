title: Uncertainty
aspect: Not knowing
line: We do not need to see the whole path to take the next step.
invite: When you catch yourself trying to predict the future this week, come back to one question: what do I need to do today?
===
This week we practise being with uncertainty. The mind dislikes not knowing. It tries to predict, to plan, to imagine every possible outcome, hoping to feel safe.

But the future is always uncertain. No amount of worrying can change that. What we can learn is to rest in not knowing, trusting that we will meet each moment when it comes, as we always have.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's notice uncertainty right here. You don't know exactly what the next sound will be. You don't know what thought will come next. You don't know exactly how this breath will feel. And yet, you are all right.
[pause 75]
Each breath arrives without you knowing its exact shape. You trust it to come. Rest in that small trust for a while.
[pause 90]
Now think of something uncertain in your life. A decision, a change, a situation that could go many ways. Notice how the mind wants to rush ahead and solve it.

Notice the body too. Perhaps there is tension, or restlessness.
[pause 90]
Imagine walking along a path through fog. You can only see a few steps ahead. You don't need to see the whole way. You only need to see the next step, and take it.
[pause 120]
Silently, say to yourself: I don't know, and that's all right. I will meet what comes, when it comes.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I open to not knowing. Breathing out, I trust this step.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. When you catch yourself trying to predict the future, come back to one simple question: What do I need to do today? Let tomorrow look after itself.
[pause 20]
[closing]
