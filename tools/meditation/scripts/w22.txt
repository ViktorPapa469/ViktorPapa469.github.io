title: Boundaries with Care
aspect: Self-respect
line: A kind no makes room for an honest yes.
invite: This week, say one gentle no to something that drains you, and notice what it makes room for.
===
This week we look at boundaries. A boundary is simply knowing where you end and others begin. What you can give, and what you cannot.

Without boundaries, we can become exhausted, resentful, or lost in other people's needs. With boundaries that are held kindly, we have more to give, not less. A riverbank does not stop the river. It gives the river its shape and strength.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by feeling the edges of the body. Notice where your skin meets the air. Where your body touches the seat. Feel the outline of yourself, here, in this space.
[pause 75]
Now notice the space around you. The air in front of you, beside you, behind you. Imagine a gentle bubble of space that belongs to you. Not a wall. Just a soft, clear edge.
[pause 90]
Think of a situation where you often give more than you can. Saying yes when you are already tired. Taking on someone else's problem as if it were your own.

Notice how your body feels when you think of it.
[pause 90]
Now imagine responding differently. Kindly, but clearly. Perhaps you say: I care about this, but I can't do it right now. Or: I need some time for myself today.

Notice what it feels like to imagine saying that. Perhaps there is fear. Perhaps relief. Both are normal.
[pause 120]
Remember: you can care deeply about others, and still look after yourself. In fact, looking after yourself is part of how you care for others well.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I honour my limits. Breathing out, I give from fullness, not from empty.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Say one gentle no to something that drains you. Then notice what that no makes room for.
[pause 20]
