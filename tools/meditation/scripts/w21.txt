title: Speaking Truthfully
aspect: Honesty
line: Kind truth heals. Unkind truth wounds. Untruth divides.
invite: Before you speak this week, ask yourself: is it true, is it kind, is it helpful? Let that pause guide your words.
===
This week we reflect on speaking truthfully. Most of us are not great liars. But we often bend the truth a little. We say we're fine when we're not. We agree when we don't. We exaggerate, or leave things out, to avoid discomfort.

Honest speech, offered with kindness, builds trust, both with others and within ourselves. This week, we practise noticing the small gaps between what we feel and what we say.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's start by being honest with ourselves, here, now. How are you really feeling at this moment? Not how you should feel, or how you'd like to feel. Just how you are.

Name it silently, as simply as you can. Tired. Calm. Uneasy. Content.
[pause 90]
Notice whether it was easy or difficult to be honest, even with yourself. Sometimes we hide things even from our own awareness.
[pause 60]
Now think of a recent moment when you didn't quite say what you meant. Perhaps you said yes when you meant no. Or you stayed quiet when you wanted to speak.

What stopped you? Fear of conflict, perhaps. Wanting to be liked. Not wanting to hurt someone.
[pause 120]
Imagine that moment again. This time, imagine saying what was true, but saying it gently. With care for the other person, and for yourself. What might you have said?
[pause 120]
Notice that truth and kindness are not enemies. The most powerful words are often both.
[pause 75]
Breathing in, I listen to what is true. Breathing out, I speak with care.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Before you speak, ask yourself three quiet questions. Is it true? Is it kind? Is it helpful? Let that small pause guide your words.
