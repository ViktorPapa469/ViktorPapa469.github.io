title: Loving-kindness
aspect: Warmth
line: Wish others well, and your own heart grows lighter.
invite: As you pass people this week, on the street, at work, at home, silently wish them well: may you be happy.
===
This week we practise loving-kindness. It is one of the oldest meditations, found in many traditions. We simply wish others well, starting with those close to us, and gently widening the circle.

It may feel awkward at first, as if we are only saying words. That's all right. Like watering a seed, the words are the water. The warmth grows in its own time.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Begin with someone it is easy to love. Someone whose face makes you smile. Picture them clearly, and silently offer these wishes:

May you be happy. May you be healthy. May you be safe. May you live with ease.
[pause 90]
Now include yourself. Picture yourself sitting here, as if you were seeing yourself from across the room. Offer the same wishes:

May I be happy. May I be healthy. May I be safe. May I live with ease.
[pause 90]
Now think of someone you hardly know. A shopkeeper, a neighbour, someone you passed today. They too have hopes and worries, just like you. Offer them the same wishes.

May you be happy. May you be healthy. May you be safe. May you live with ease.
[pause 90]
If you feel ready, think of someone you find difficult. Not the hardest person in your life. Just someone who irritates you a little. Remember that they too want to be happy, even if they go about it clumsily.

May you be happy. May you be at ease.
[pause 120]
And finally, let the circle open to everyone, everywhere. People waking up, people falling asleep, people struggling, people celebrating.

May all beings be happy. May all beings be at ease.
[pause 120]
This week, here is your invitation. As you pass people, on the street, at work, at home, silently wish them well. May you be happy. Notice how it changes the way you see them, and the way you feel.
[pause 20]
[closing]
