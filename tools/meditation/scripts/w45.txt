title: Silence
aspect: Inner quiet
line: In silence, we hear what noise has been hiding.
invite: Each day this week, spend fifteen minutes in silence: no music, no podcasts, no screens. Just the sounds of the world.
===
This week we enter silence. Our days are full of noise. Not only sounds, but messages, news, opinions, music, voices in our heads. We rarely experience true quiet.

Silence can feel uncomfortable at first. But it is in silence that we hear our own thoughts clearly, feel our own feelings, and rest more deeply than sleep. This week, I will speak less, and leave you more space to listen.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's enter silence together. For the next while, I will only speak occasionally. Simply rest in the quiet, and come back to the breath whenever the mind wanders.
[pause 150]
Notice that silence is not empty. It is full of small sounds, sensations, and a deep, spacious stillness.
[pause 150]
If thoughts are loud, let them speak, and then let them fade back into the quiet.
[pause 150]
Rest in the silence beneath everything.
[pause 120]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, spend fifteen minutes in silence. No music, no podcasts, no screens. Just you, and the sounds of the world.
