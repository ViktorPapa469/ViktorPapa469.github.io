title: Joy for Others
aspect: Shared happiness
line: Another person's joy takes nothing away from yours.
invite: When you hear good news about someone this week, pause and let yourself be glad for them, fully, before anything else.
===
This week we practise a quality that is easy to overlook: being glad for others. When a friend succeeds, part of us is happy for them. But often, another part quietly compares. Why not me? Am I falling behind?

There is an old practice of training the heart to rejoice in other people's good fortune. It turns the world into a place full of joys we can share, rather than a race we are losing.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with someone you love. Picture them at a happy moment. Laughing, perhaps, or proud of something they've done. Let yourself feel glad, simply because they are happy.
[pause 90]
Notice that their joy doesn't cost you anything. It's not a pie that gets smaller when it's shared. If anything, it grows.
[pause 60]
Now think of someone whose success has made you feel a little envious. Perhaps a colleague, a friend, someone you see online. Notice the envy, without judging it. It's a very human feeling. It often points to something we wish for ourselves.
[pause 90]
Now, gently, see if you can also wish them well. Silently say: I'm glad for your happiness. May it continue.

It may feel forced at first. That's all right. You are planting a seed.
[pause 120]
Finally, think of all the joys happening right now, all over the world. Babies being born. Friends reuniting. Someone hearing good news. Someone laughing until they cry. Let yourself rest in this enormous field of happiness, and feel glad that it exists.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I see your joy. Breathing out, I share in it.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. When you hear good news about someone, pause and let yourself be glad for them, fully, before anything else. Let their joy become yours as well.
[pause 20]
[closing]
