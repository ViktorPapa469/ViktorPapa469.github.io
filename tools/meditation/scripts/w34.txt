title: Failure as Teacher
aspect: Learning
line: Every fall carries a lesson, if we stay long enough to hear it.
invite: This week, when something goes wrong, write down one thing it taught you before you move on.
===
This week we look at failure. Few words carry so much fear. We avoid trying new things, because we might fail. We replay old failures for years.

But every skill you have was built on many small failures. You fell many times before you could walk. Failure is not the opposite of success. It is part of the path towards it. This week, we practise letting failure become a teacher, rather than a judge.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by remembering a child learning to walk. They stand, they wobble, they fall. And then they laugh, and get up again. They don't decide they are bad at walking. They just keep trying.

Picture that child for a moment. Feel the simple courage of it.
[pause 90]
Now think of something in your own life that didn't go as planned. A project, an exam, a relationship, a goal. Notice what feelings come up. Disappointment, embarrassment, frustration. Let them be here.
[pause 90]
Breathe with those feelings. Then, gently, shift your attention to a different question. Not, what does this say about me? But, what did this teach me?
[pause 120]
Perhaps it taught you something practical. Perhaps something about yourself. Perhaps simply that you can survive disappointment. Let the lesson rise naturally.
[pause 120]
Silently, say to yourself: I am learning. Every attempt is part of the path.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I accept what happened. Breathing out, I keep the lesson.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. When something goes wrong, write down one thing it taught you before you move on. Let even your mistakes give you something.
[pause 20]
[closing]
