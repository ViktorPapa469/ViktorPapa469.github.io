title: Courage
aspect: Meeting fear
line: Courage is not the absence of fear. It is kindness walking beside it.
invite: This week, do one small thing that scares you a little, and let fear come along for the ride.
===
This week we sit with fear, and find the courage that lives alongside it. Fear is a natural part of being alive. It tries to protect us. But sometimes it grows too loud, and keeps us from living fully.

Courage is not having no fear. It is feeling the fear, and still choosing what matters. This week, we learn to sit with fear gently, so that it no longer has to shout.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by feeling steady. Notice your feet on the ground, or your body on the seat. Feel the solid support beneath you. This is your foundation.
[pause 60]
Now bring to mind something that makes you a little anxious. A conversation you're avoiding. A step you're afraid to take. Choose something mild, not overwhelming.

Notice where fear shows up in the body. Perhaps a fluttering in the stomach, a tightness in the chest, a quickness in the breath.
[pause 90]
Instead of pushing it away, be curious. What does it feel like, exactly? Hot or cold? Moving or still? Let it be there, while you breathe slowly.
[pause 90]
Place a hand on the place where you feel the fear, if you like. Imagine speaking to it kindly: I know you're trying to protect me. Thank you. I can take it from here.
[pause 120]
Now imagine taking that step you are afraid of. Picture yourself doing it, with fear beside you, not in charge. Notice that you can feel afraid, and still move forward.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I feel the fear. Breathing out, I find my ground.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. Do one small thing that scares you a little. Let fear come along for the ride, but keep your own hands on the wheel.
[pause 20]
[closing]
