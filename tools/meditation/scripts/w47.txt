title: Abundance
aspect: Noticing what we have
line: Look closely, and life is overflowing.
invite: When you feel lack this week, pause and list three things you already have in abundance.
===
This week we explore abundance. Earlier this year, we practised the word enough. Now we go a step further, and notice that in many ways, life is overflowing.

The mind is trained to count what is missing. But look closely at almost any moment, and you will find a richness we usually overlook. Breath without limit. Countless colours. People who care. Lessons everywhere. This week, we practise seeing that richness.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin with the breath. Notice that air is always here. You never run out. With each breath, you receive exactly as much as you need, and more is waiting.
[pause 90]
Now think of the abundance of your senses. How many colours have you seen today? How many sounds have you heard? How many tastes and textures? Countless. Every moment is full of them.
[pause 90]
Think of the abundance of people in your life. Not only close friends, but everyone who has helped, taught, smiled, served or simply shared the same street with you.
[pause 90]
Think of the abundance within you. All you have learned. All you have survived. All the love you have given and received.
[pause 120]
Let the feeling of fullness grow. Not pride, not greed. Just a quiet sense of being richly supplied by life.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I receive. Breathing out, I overflow.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. When you feel lack, pause and list three things you already have in abundance. Let gratitude widen into a sense of plenty.
[pause 20]
[closing]
