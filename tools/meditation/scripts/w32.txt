title: Everything Changes
aspect: Impermanence
line: Because nothing stays the same, every moment is precious.
invite: Each day this week, notice one thing that is changing: a sky, a mood, a plant, a face. Let it remind you to treasure today.
===
This week we reflect on impermanence. Everything changes. Seasons turn. Children grow. Moods come and go. Even the hardest days end, and even the best days pass.

This can feel sad, or even frightening. But it is also a great freedom. Because things change, pain does not last forever. Because things change, growth is possible. And because things change, this moment, right now, is precious.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's watch change happening right here, in the breath. Each in-breath begins, grows, and ends. Each out-breath begins, flows, and ends. No two are exactly alike.
[pause 75]
Now notice sensations in the body. Tingling, warmth, pressure. Watch closely, and you may see that they are constantly shifting. Getting stronger, fading, moving.
[pause 90]
Notice sounds, too. Each one arises, and passes away. Nothing stays.
[pause 75]
Now think of a difficulty you were facing a year ago. Notice how much has changed since then. Some things got better. Some got different. You got through it.
[pause 90]
Now think of something you love, that you know will not last forever. A season of life, a person, a place. Let yourself feel both the love and the tenderness of knowing it will change. Let that tenderness make you more present, not more afraid.
[pause 120]
Rest in the flow of this moment, without grasping, without pushing away. Like sitting on the bank of a river, watching the water pass.
[pause 120]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, notice one thing that is changing. A sky, a mood, a plant, a face. Let it remind you to treasure today, exactly as it is.
[pause 20]
[closing]
