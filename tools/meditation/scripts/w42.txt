title: Wholeheartedness
aspect: Doing one thing fully
line: Whatever you do, do it with your whole heart, and the ordinary becomes whole.
invite: Each day this week, choose one task and do only that, with full attention, from start to finish.
===
This week we practise wholeheartedness. So much of the day is spent half here. Eating while scrolling. Listening while planning. Working while worrying about something else.

When we give one thing our whole attention, something changes. The task becomes lighter, the work becomes better, and we feel more alive. This is the heart of focus: not forcing the mind, but gathering it, gently, into one place.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's practise doing one thing fully, right now. The one thing is breathing. Give this breath your whole attention. Its beginning, its middle, its end. Nothing else matters for these few seconds.
[pause 90]
When the mind divides, part here and part elsewhere, gently gather it back. Like gathering scattered leaves into a single pile.
[pause 120]
Now imagine a task you will do later today or tomorrow. Something ordinary. Washing dishes, writing an email, studying a page, cooking a meal.

Imagine doing it wholeheartedly. Your body relaxed. Your mind clear. Your attention resting completely on what is in front of you. No rush to finish. Just this.
[pause 120]
Notice how it feels to imagine it. Perhaps calmer. Perhaps more satisfying. When we are wholly present, even simple things become complete in themselves.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I gather myself. Breathing out, I give my whole heart to this.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. Each day, choose one task and do only that, with full attention, from start to finish. You might use a Fovea session for it. Notice what changes.
