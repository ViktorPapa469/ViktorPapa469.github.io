title: Belonging
aspect: Connection
line: You do not have to earn your place in the world.
invite: This week, reach out to one person you have lost touch with, just to say you were thinking of them.
===
This week we reflect on belonging. Deep down, everyone wants to feel that they belong. To a family, a group of friends, a community, a place.

Sometimes we feel we have to earn belonging, by being useful, or impressive, or agreeable. But true belonging begins with knowing that you are part of life simply because you are alive. Like every tree belongs to the forest, and every wave belongs to the sea.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by feeling the ground beneath you. The earth is holding you, right now, without asking anything in return. You belong to this earth, as much as any tree or stone or bird.
[pause 90]
Now think of a place where you have felt you belonged. A home, a group, a moment with people who accepted you as you are. Remember the feeling of being welcomed.
[pause 90]
Notice where that feeling sits in the body. Let it grow a little, as you breathe.
[pause 75]
Now think of the times you have felt like an outsider. Not fitting in. Being misunderstood. Gently acknowledge how painful that can be. Many people, everywhere, have felt the same way. Even in that feeling, you are not alone.
[pause 120]
Silently, you might say to yourself: I belong here. I do not have to earn my place. And then, think of someone who might need to hear that from you.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I am held by the earth. Breathing out, I make room for others.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Reach out to one person you have lost touch with, just to say you were thinking of them. A small message can remind someone that they belong too.
[pause 20]
[closing]
