title: The Path Continues
aspect: Integration
line: There is no finish line. Only the next step, taken with care.
invite: Choose one practice from this year to keep, and begin it again tomorrow morning.
===
This is the final week of our year together. Fifty-two weeks. Fifty-two small doorways into a more awake, more gentle life.

But there is no finish line in this practice. Like the breath, it continues. The end of this year is simply the beginning of the next. This week, we gather what we have learned, and choose how to carry it forward.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by arriving, just as we did in the very first week. Feel the body here. Feel the breath. Silently say: I am here. This is now.
[pause 90]
Notice how these words feel to you today, compared with the first time you heard them. Perhaps more familiar. Perhaps more true.
[pause 90]
Now let one practice from this year come to mind. The one that has helped you most. Perhaps the breath as home. Perhaps kindness to yourself. Perhaps listening, or gratitude, or beginning again. Let it choose you.
[pause 120]
Imagine carrying this practice into the year ahead. Picture a morning, an ordinary day, a difficult moment, and see yourself using it. Simply. Naturally. Like an old friend at your side.
[pause 120]
Finally, take a moment to thank yourself for this year of practice. For every time you sat down, every breath you noticed, every time you came back. These moments have shaped you, whether you can see it or not.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I carry what I have learned. Breathing out, I walk on, with care.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Choose one practice from this year to keep, and begin it again tomorrow morning. And whenever you are ready, you can return to week one, and begin the journey again, with new eyes.
[pause 20]
[closing]
