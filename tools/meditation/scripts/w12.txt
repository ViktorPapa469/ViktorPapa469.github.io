title: Rest
aspect: Renewal
line: Rest is not the opposite of growth. It is part of it.
invite: Give yourself one true rest each day this week: ten minutes with no phone, no task, and no guilt.
===
This week we practise rest. Many of us find it strangely hard to rest. Even when we stop, the mind keeps working, making lists, replaying the day, feeling guilty about what is undone.

But nothing in nature grows without rest. Fields lie fallow. Trees let go of their leaves. Night follows day. This week, we give ourselves permission to rest, not as a reward for finishing everything, but as something we need to keep going at all.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by letting the body become heavy. Feel the weight of your legs, sinking down. The weight of your arms. The weight of your head, resting on your neck.

Let gravity do the work. You don't need to hold anything up right now.
[pause 75]
Let the breath slow down on its own. Not by forcing it. Just by allowing the out-breath to be as long as it wants to be.
[pause 75]
Now notice any part of you that is still busy. Perhaps the mind is planning. Perhaps a part of you is watching the clock. Gently say to that part: You can rest too. Nothing needs to be solved right now.
[pause 90]
Imagine lying in a field on a warm afternoon. The grass is soft beneath you. Above, a few clouds drift slowly by. There is nowhere you have to go. No one needs anything from you.
[pause 120]
If guilt arises, the feeling that you should be doing something more useful, notice it, and let it pass like one of those clouds. Rest is useful. Rest is how the body repairs, and how the mind makes room for what is new.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I let myself be held. Breathing out, I let go of doing.
[pause 90]
This week, here is your invitation. Give yourself one true rest each day: ten minutes with no phone, no task, and no guilt. Sit, lie down, look out of a window. Let it be enough.
[pause 20]
[closing]
