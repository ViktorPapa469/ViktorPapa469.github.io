title: Simplicity
aspect: Less, but better
line: When we carry less, we can feel more.
invite: This week, let go of one thing each day: an object you don't need, a commitment you can drop, a habit that clutters your time.
===
This week we explore simplicity. Modern life tends to pile up. More possessions, more commitments, more notifications, more things to keep track of. Each one asks for a little of our attention, and our energy.

Simplicity doesn't mean having nothing. It means keeping what truly matters, and letting the rest go, so that we have room to breathe. Room to notice. Room to live.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by simplifying this moment. There is only one thing to do right now: sit and breathe. Nothing else. Let everything else fall away for a while.
[pause 90]
Notice how much lighter it feels when there is only one thing to do.
[pause 60]
Now think of your life as a room. Imagine walking into it. Is it spacious, or crowded? What fills it? Possessions, plans, worries, obligations, screens.
[pause 90]
Imagine gently clearing the room. One by one, setting down things you don't need. Not throwing everything away. Just keeping what you love, and what you truly use.
[pause 120]
Notice the space that opens up. What would you do with that space? Perhaps more rest. More time with people you love. More quiet.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I let go of what I don't need. Breathing out, I make room for what matters.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Let go of one thing each day. An object you don't need. A commitment you can drop. A habit that clutters your time. Notice how each small release makes life a little lighter.
[pause 20]
[closing]
