title: Gratitude
aspect: Thankfulness
line: Gratitude turns what we have into enough.
invite: Each morning this week, before you look at your phone, name one thing you are grateful for.
===
Welcome to the second season of this practice. For the next thirteen weeks, we turn towards other people, and towards the ways our lives are woven together. We begin with gratitude.

Gratitude is not pretending everything is fine. It is noticing, even in difficult times, the many gifts we did not create ourselves. The food that someone grew. The kindness of a stranger. The breath that arrives without our asking.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin close to home. Think of something simple from today that you are grateful for. A warm drink. A moment of sunlight. A comfortable place to sit right now.

Let yourself feel the gratitude, not just think it. Notice where it lives in the body.
[pause 75]
Now think of a person who has helped you, perhaps long ago. A teacher, a relative, a friend who stood by you. Picture their face. Remember what they did.
[pause 90]
Silently, say thank you to them. You might imagine them hearing it. Notice what happens in your chest as you say it.
[pause 90]
Now widen the circle. Think of all the people you will never meet who made your day possible. The farmers who grew your food. The people who built the roads, the buildings, the device in your hand. Thousands of hands, working for you, today.
[pause 120]
And finally, turn gratitude towards yourself. Thank your body for carrying you. Thank your mind for trying so hard. Thank yourself for showing up to this practice.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I receive. Breathing out, I give thanks.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. Each morning, before you look at your phone, name one thing you are grateful for. Let the first thought of the day be one of thanks.
[pause 20]
[closing]
