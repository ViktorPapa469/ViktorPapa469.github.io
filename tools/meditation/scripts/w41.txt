title: Purpose
aspect: Direction
line: Purpose is found less in what we achieve and more in how we give.
invite: This week, notice the moments when you feel most useful and alive, and write a few of them down.
===
This week we reflect on purpose. Many people feel pressure to find one great purpose for their life, and worry that they haven't found it yet.

But purpose is often quieter than that. It shows up in small moments, when our strengths meet someone's need. When we lose track of time doing something that feels right. When we give something only we can give. This week, we listen for those moments.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by remembering a time you felt deeply alive. Fully engaged, as if you were doing exactly what you were meant to be doing. It might be something big, or very ordinary. Let the memory return in detail.
[pause 120]
What were you doing? Who were you with? What made it feel meaningful? Let yourself notice the qualities of that moment.
[pause 90]
Now think of something that comes naturally to you. A strength others often notice. Perhaps listening, organising, making people laugh, explaining things, creating beauty. You may not even see it as special. But it is a gift.
[pause 90]
Now consider: Who might benefit from that gift? What small need in the world around you could it meet?
[pause 120]
Let any ideas come and go without needing to decide anything. Purpose often grows slowly, from paying attention.
[pause 90]
Breathing in, I notice what makes me come alive. Breathing out, I offer it to the world.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Notice the moments when you feel most useful and alive, and write a few of them down. Over time, they will show you a path.
[pause 20]
[closing]
