title: Time
aspect: Living each day
line: We don't have time. We have moments, and this is one.
invite: Each morning this week, ask: if today were my only day, what would I want to give it to? Then give it at least one thing.
===
This week we reflect on time. We talk about time as if it were money. We spend it, save it, waste it, run out of it. And so we often feel we never have enough.

But time is not really something we have. It is something we live. Each day is made of moments, and the only moment we can ever live is this one. This week, we practise being here for the days we are given.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's notice time passing right here. Each breath takes a few seconds. In the time of one breath, your heart beats several times. Somewhere, a flower opens a little more. A child grows a tiny bit taller.
[pause 90]
Notice that you cannot hold onto this moment. It is already becoming the next. And yet, it is fully here.
[pause 90]
Think of how you spent yesterday. Not to judge it. Just notice: which moments were you truly present for? Which ones passed while your mind was elsewhere?
[pause 120]
Now imagine tomorrow. If it were your only day, what would you want to give it to? A person? A piece of work? A walk? A rest? A kind word?
[pause 120]
Let that choice become a quiet intention.
[pause 60]
Breathing in, I am here for this moment. Breathing out, I let it be enough.
[pause 60]
This week, here is your invitation. Each morning, ask: If today were my only day, what would I want to give it to? Then give it at least one thing.
[pause 20]
[closing]
