title: Grief and Loss
aspect: Holding sorrow
line: Grief is love with nowhere to go. Let it have somewhere.
invite: This week, give your sorrow a little time each day: a walk, a few written lines, a quiet minute with a photograph.
===
This week we turn gently towards grief and loss. Everyone loses things. People we love. Places, health, hopes, chapters of our lives. Grief is not a problem to be solved. It is the natural response of a heart that has loved.

Please take care with this meditation. If you are carrying a very fresh or heavy loss, you are welcome to stay with the breath the whole time, or to stop whenever you need to. And please remember that sorrow is easier to carry with others. Reaching out to someone you trust, or to a counsellor, is a strength, not a weakness.
[pause 15]
[anchor]
Let's begin by creating a safe place inside. Feel the support beneath you. Place a hand on your heart, or on your belly, wherever feels comforting. Let the breath be soft.
[pause 75]
Now, if it feels right, bring to mind a loss. It can be small, or it can be one you have carried for a long time. You don't need to go into the whole story. Just let it be present, gently, at the edge of your attention.
[pause 90]
Notice how grief shows up in the body. Heaviness. An ache. Tears, perhaps. Whatever is here, let it be here. You don't have to be strong right now.
[pause 90]
Say to yourself, softly: This is sorrow. Sorrow is part of love. I can hold this, a little at a time.
[pause 120]
Now remember something good about what you lost. A memory, a gift it gave you, a way it changed you. Let gratitude and sorrow sit side by side. They are not opposites. They often come together.
[pause 120]
Breathing in, I hold what hurts. Breathing out, I remember what was loved.
[pause 75]
This week, here is your invitation. Give your sorrow a little time each day. A walk, a few written lines, a quiet minute with a photograph. Let grief move, instead of keeping it locked away. And let someone you trust walk alongside you.
[pause 20]
[closing]
